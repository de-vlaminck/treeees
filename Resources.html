    Tree Selection & Placement

    The right tree, for the right place will save you headache and heartbreak. Consider what characteristics are important to you; mature size, wildlife value, climate reslience, fall color, flowers, fruiting, seedless, etc. Also consider your available spaces; are there nearby structures, existing trees, utilities, or future plans for development. It can be helpful to start either at site preference or tree preference and meet somewhere in the middle.
