Projects

Tree Sales

In an effort to reduce barriers to tree planting and to increase canopy cover throughout the metro we work with cities to offer low-cost trees. We distribute about 5,000 low cost trees to residents annually to plant on their own! We also work with residents and commercial property owners to plant trees free and low-cost trees for them, to further reduce barriers. We plant over 800 trees on private property annually!

GIS Mapping

I am working to improve our efficiency and project quality by incorporating GIS into more projects. Below is an example interactive map created for residents to determine their eligibility for a grant funded program. I am interested in expanding my skills to provide interactive tools for the public to visualize trees in their community and understand their benefits!

Tree Preservation

In 2025-2026 I had the opportunity to help shape the development of the City of St Paul's Tree Preservation Ordninance, through an advisory group of residents, city staff, and industry professionals. The new Tree Preservation Ordinance was passed in July 2026 and will provide guidance on preservation requirements during city sponsored projects affecting public trees.
